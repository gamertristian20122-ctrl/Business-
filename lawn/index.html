The Omaha lawn year

Omaha is USDA zone 5b. Most lawns here are Kentucky bluegrass and tall fescue, which grow hardest in spring and fall and slow down in July heat. Here's when each job happens.

Spring Mar–May
Cleanup once the snow is gone. Pre-emergent goes down when the soil reaches 55°F, about when the forsythia blooms in mid-April. First mow is usually the third week of April.
Summer Jun–Aug
We raise the deck to 4 inches in July heat. Lawns need about an inch of water a week. If it browns in a dry spell, it's dormant, and it comes back.
Fall Sep–Nov
The most important season. Aerate and seed by early October, then leaves come off before they mat. Last mow goes a little shorter, at 3 inches, so snow mold has less to grab.
Winter Dec–Feb
Plow trucks run once 2 inches fall. We keep salt off the grass edges and stay off frozen turf, because walking on it breaks the blades.

Things people ask before signing up

Something not here? Text (402) 555-0148. Sam usually answers within the hour.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Mowing is week to week. Text us to stop any time. The only thing with a set price for the season is the snow plan, and you can still cancel that before December 1.

What happens when it rains on my day?

We don't mow soaked grass, because it ruts the lawn and clumps. Your visit moves to the next dry day, usually within 48 hours, and Saturday morning is our catch-up. You'll get a text either way.

I have a dog. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most of our yards have one. Put the dog inside on your mowing day, and we'll text you when we're 20 minutes out. After a fertilizer round, keep pets off for about 24 hours or until the grass is dry. The yard flag tells you when.

Do I need to be home? What about my gate?

You don't need to be home. Give us the gate code or tell us where the latch is, and we'll save it on your account. We check the gate is latched before we leave, every time.

How does billing work?

You get one invoice by email on the 1st for last month's visits. Pay by card, Venmo or check. If we skip a week for drought or rain we can't make up, you don't pay for it.

Do you bag the clippings?

We mulch them back into the lawn. It feeds the soil and doesn't cause thatch. If it's grown long after a rainy week, we bag that cut so it doesn't leave clumps.

Can I skip a week when I'm on vacation?

You can, but we'd suggest keeping the visit. Skipping a week in May means the grass grows past 6 inches, and the next cut stresses it. Most people keep us coming while they're away so the house doesn't look empty.

Is the weed control safe for kids?

We use products labeled for home lawns, applied by a licensed applicator at the label rate. We spot-spray weeds instead of blanketing the whole yard. Once it's dry, kids and pets can be back on it.
