# Website demos

Sample websites for Omaha small businesses, published with GitHub Pages.
The businesses are made up; names, phone numbers (555) and reviews are placeholders.

- `detailing/` – car detailing, black, real studio video, inspection-light photo, prices by vehicle size
- `cleaning/` – house cleaning, black, told as one cleaning day
- `lawn-dark/` – lawn care, black with green accent, told in chapters
- `lawn/` – lawn care, paper and green
- `lawn-seasons/` – lawn & snow, switches between seasons

Photos: Unsplash (Unsplash License). The detailing demo's photos and video are by Tima Miroshnichenko on Pexels (Pexels License).
