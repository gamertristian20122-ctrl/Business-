2016. Eleven yards and a push mower.

Sam Kowalski was sixteen, and his grandpa's mower was sitting in a garage in Millard.

That summer he cut eleven yards on his own block for $20 each. He showed up on the same day every week, closed every gate behind him and edged the walks even when nobody asked. By August, the neighbors' parents were calling.

Ten seasons later it's a crew of six with three trucks. Sam still runs the Tuesday route himself. The rules from that first summer haven't changed.

Spring Mar–May
Cleanup once the snow is gone. Pre-emergent goes down when the soil hits 55°F, about when the forsythia blooms in mid-April. First mow is usually the third week of April.
Summer Jun–Aug
The deck goes up to 4 inches in July heat. Lawns need about an inch of water a week. Brown in a dry spell means dormant, and it comes back.
Fall Sep–Nov
The most important season. Aerate and seed by early October, then get the leaves off before they mat. The last mow is a little shorter, at 3 inches, so snow mold has less to grab.
Winter Dec–Feb
Plows roll once 2 inches fall. We keep salt off the grass edges and stay off frozen turf, because walking on it breaks the blades.

Questions people ask first

Something not here? Text (402) 555-0148. Sam usually answers within the hour.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Mowing is week to week, and you can text us to stop any time. Only the snow plan has a season price, and you can cancel that before December 1.

What happens when it rains on my day?

We don't mow soaked grass, because it ruts the lawn and clumps. Your visit moves to the next dry day, usually within 48 hours, and Saturday morning is our catch-up. You'll get a text either way.

I have a dog. Is that a problem?

Most of our yards have one. Keep the dog inside on your mowing day, and we'll text when we're 20 minutes out. After a fertilizer round, keep pets off until the grass is dry. The yard flag tells you when.

Do I need to be home? What about my gate?

You don't need to be home. Give us the gate code or tell us where the latch is, and we'll save it on your account. We check that the gate is latched before we leave, every time.

How does billing work?

One invoice by email on the 1st for last month's visits. Pay by card, Venmo or check. If we skip a week for drought or a rainout we can't make up, you don't pay for it.

Do you bag the clippings?

We mulch them back into the lawn, which feeds the soil and doesn't cause thatch. If the grass got long after a rainy week, we bag that cut so it doesn't leave clumps.

Can I skip a week when I'm on vacation?

You can, but we'd suggest keeping it. A skipped week in May lets the grass get past 6 inches, and the next cut stresses it. A mowed yard also makes the house look lived in.

Is the weed control safe for kids?

We use products labeled for home lawns, applied by a licensed applicator at the label rate. We spot-spray weeds instead of blanketing the yard. Once it's dry, kids and pets can be back on it.
